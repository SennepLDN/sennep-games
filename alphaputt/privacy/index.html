Privacy Policy

Alphaputt policy — Last updated: June 2026

Alphaputt is our typographic take on crazy golf — beautifully crafted, and just as carefully minimal with your data. This page explains what we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

What we collect

We collect a small amount of data to make the game work and improve it over time.

This may include:

  • Basic device information (model, OS version)
  • Gameplay data (progress, levels completed, scores)
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • Anonymous identifiers used for analytics

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name or email unless you choose to contact us directly.

How we use it

We use this data to:

  • Make sure the game runs smoothly
  • Understand how players interact with the game
  • Fix bugs and improve gameplay

Third-party services

We use a small number of trusted platform tools to help us run and improve Alphaputt, such as crash and performance reporting and anonymous analytics.

We do not use third-party advertising or marketing-attribution services.

Any data collected by these tools is handled in accordance with their own privacy policies.

How long we keep data

We keep data only for as long as we need it.

  • Crash reports and performance data: typically up to 12 months
  • Analytics data: typically up to 24 months
  • Anything you send us by email: kept until the conversation is resolved, then deleted on request

Your choices

You can limit tracking and analytics through your device settings.

You also have the right to:

  • Ask what data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Ask us to stop using it for certain purposes

To make any of these requests, email matt@sennep.com.

Children

Alphaputt is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has shared data with us, please email matt@sennep.com and we will delete it.

Contact

If you have any questions or requests, you can contact us at:
matt@sennep.com

Updates

We may update this policy as the game evolves. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
